Q1 key findings (each one has a Q2 solution in section 5)

  1. Q1 volume was 2,807,747 CFT, 71.5% of the 3,925,000 CFT target. July 62.9%, August 81.7%, September 69.4%; the run-rate is far below the target.
  2. Growth is price-led, not volume-led. Net sales are flat (+0.1%) while volume is down 15.7%; realisation rose 18.8% to BDT 370.4/CFT.
  3. August was loss-making. Net income was -BDT 15.1M against a +BDT 2.5M budget; gross margin was BDT 55.8M vs BDT 84.5M budget.
  4. Plant profitability is uneven. August NP margin: Rupganj +4.8%, Dhour -3.3%, Gazipur -2.8%, Narayanganj -35.5%, Chittagong -62.2%; September is negative at every plant.
  5. The portfolio is concentrated. 3500/4000/4500/5000 PSI PCC are 84.2% of Sep net sales; the growth grade M20/3000 PSI is only 2.1% of Sep and 3.0% of FY25-26.
  6. Customer concentration is high. The top 15 customers are BDT 33.06 Cr, 31.8% of YTD sales, led by A One Polar, Bashundhara City and Akij Ceramics.
  7. Akij is the price leader in a price-sensitive market. Blended BDT 370/CFT vs NDE 340-345, Shah 345-350, Bashundhara 340-345 and Crown 355-360; volume is paying for the premium.
  8. Market position: 8% total market (Marketing Pack; no DWH source) and 12.4% of listed RMC deliveries, rank #5; the gap to #4 Bashundhara is 150,000 CFT/month.
  9. The website is not a sales channel. The 14 Oct 2026 audit finds placeholders, a wrong company name, cement-heavy content and no quote, booking or plant locator.
  10. Brand spend is under-executed. Q1 actual BDT 573,500 vs BDT 2,205,008 plan (26.0%): OOH over (567,000 vs 350,008), Gifts 6,500, ATL/Digital/BTL zero. Q2 to date: 239,000 Gift Item (4.7% of Q2 plan).

Capacity utilisation, top five

Akij 51.9% is the highest; Shah 23.6% has the most headroom. Utilisation is a strength today but a constraint on the +49% FY27 volume plan.

What the competitor data says

  • Akij is the price leader. BDT 370/CFT vs 340-360 for the volume leaders; the 15.7% volume decline is the cost of that premium.
  • The gap to #4 is bridgeable. 150,000 CFT/month is about 14% of Akij's current volume; #3 Crown needs 300,000.
  • Fleet depth is the moat. Shah and NDE run 212-263 mixers and 40-42 pumps; Akij has 94 mixers and 28 pumps.
  • Coverage is comparable in Dhaka, Narayanganj, Gazipur and Chattogram; the IHB/dealer route is the missing channel, not plant footprint.

  • Contact page is broken: refers to "Ajik Shipping", lists a placeholder phone +1234567890 and an unfinished Dhaka office block.
